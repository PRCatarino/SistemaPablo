PRODUCTION #8821

Summer Bloom Silk Collection

Briefing for high-density jacquard weaving patterns

Status In Progress

Create a series of intricate botanical patterns inspired by Mediterranean summer gardens. The designs must translate effectively into silk-blend jacquard with a focus on tactile depth and subtle metallic thread highlights.

  • 2400 hook electronic jacquard
  • 100% Silk weft / Poly-Cotton warp
  • Maximum 8 color weft rotation
  • 3 Unique repeat patterns (64x64cm)
  • Digital simulation for warp tension
  • Color-way cards (Morning, Dusk, Midnight)
